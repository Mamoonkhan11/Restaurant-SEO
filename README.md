# 🍔 QR-Curve 

Welcome to **QR-Curve**, the ultimate digital menu platform designed to skyrocket your restaurant's online presence, engage your customers, and boost your sales effortlessly! 

## 🌟 What is QR-Curve?
Gone are the days of sticky, outdated physical menus. QR-Curve gives restaurant owners the power to create a stunning, real-time digital menu that customers can access instantly by scanning a beautifully generated QR code placed right at their tables.

## 🚀 Key Features

### For Your Customers:
- **Lightning-Fast Digital Menu**: A highly responsive, mobile-first menu interface that looks incredible.
- **Smart Search & Categories**: Easily find the exact dish you are craving using our real-time search bar and scrollable category tags.
- **Bestseller Badges**: Let the data speak for itself! Dishes automatically earn "Bestseller" badges based on weekly customer views.
- **WhatsApp Ordering**: Found something you like? Tap the WhatsApp button to instantly message the restaurant and ask about home delivery!
- **Easy Sharing**: Love the menu? Hit the "Share Menu" button to instantly forward the menu to friends and family.

### For Restaurant Owners (Admin Dashboard):
- **Live Menu Management**: Add, edit, or mark dishes as 'Out of Stock' in real-time. Changes reflect instantly without needing to reprint anything.
- **Custom Branding**: Make the menu yours. Upload your brand logo and select your primary theme color—the entire platform instantly adapts to match your brand identity!
- **High-Res QR Codes**: Generate and download crisp, table-ready QR codes complete with your logo embedded right in the center.
- **Powerful Analytics**: Monitor what's hot and what's not. 
   - **Weekly Resets**: "Best Seller" tracking resets every Monday, so you always know what's trending *right now*.
   - **Estimated Revenue**: Track your potential monthly revenue based on QR scans and your Average Order Value (AOV).
   - **Historical Context**: Click on any metric card to see a breakdown of your performance over the last 3 months.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack
QR-Curve is built for speed and reliability, leveraging modern web development tools:
- **Framework**: [Next.js](https://nextjs.org/) (React)
- **Styling**: [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/) for beautiful, responsive design
- **Database & Auth**: [Supabase](https://supabase.com/) for real-time data sync, authentication, and image storage
- **Icons**: Lucide React
- **Animations**: Framer Motion

## ⚙️ Getting Started (Local Development)

1. **Clone the Repository**
2. **Install Dependencies**: `npm install`
3. **Setup Supabase**: 
   - Create a `.env.local` file with your `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL` and `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY`.
4. **Run the Server**: `npm run dev`
5. **Visit the Platform**: Open `http://localhost:3000` to start exploring!
